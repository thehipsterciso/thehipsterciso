### SCF Machine Learning
ML-driven maturity and financial impact modeling built on the Secure Controls
Framework — a meta-framework spanning 100+ cybersecurity and privacy laws,
regulations, and industry standards across 34 control domains.

Most compliance programs produce a posture score. This produces a business
case. The models connect control coverage and maturity levels to quantified
outcomes — operational risk reduction, regulatory exposure, and the relationship
between security investment and revenue protection. Which gaps carry actual
financial consequence. Where additional investment returns measurable value.
Where it doesn't.

Built for executives who need to defend a security budget in P&L terms and for
boards who need to understand what they are actually buying when they approve one.

[securecontrolsframework](https://github.com/thehipsterciso/securecontrolsframework)

### hc-cdaio-kg
A working demonstration of what enterprise due diligence looks like when AI handles
collection and a practitioner provides the framework. Built as a CMU CDAIO program
deliverable on a public technology company — from scratch, from public sources only.

2,900+ entities. 7,600+ relationships. 401 structured analysis documents. 10-stage
due diligence covering corporate structure, business model, competitive position,
operating model, financial reality, technology platform, data maturity, risk posture,
and resilience. Every claim tagged FACT, INFERENCE, or HYPOTHESIS with a source citation.
Every stage validated before the next one opened.

The point is not the specific company. The point is that the traditional model — six
weeks, a dedicated team, and a half-million dollar engagement fee for a PDF nobody can
interrogate — is no longer the only way to answer these questions. This is what the
alternative looks like when someone applies rigor to it.

[hc-cdaio-kg](https://github.com/thehipsterciso/hc-cdaio-kg)

### hc-claude-osint
Multi-agent organizational intelligence system. 13 agents across 4 execution phases —
Architect, 10 domain specialists running in parallel, dual independent analysts, and
a synthesizing reviewer producing a single unified report.

Built for situations where you need to understand an organization before you engage it.
Pre-acquisition diligence. Partnership evaluation. Competitive positioning. Market entry.
The question it answers: what is actually true about this company, sourced from everything
they have made public, assembled in one place.

Specialists cover corporate structure and ownership, funding history and investor mapping,
executive and team composition, technology stack and vendor relationships, competitive
landscape, strategic hiring signals, government contracts, M&A activity, and public
sentiment across review platforms and community forums.

Passive only. No active scanning. All findings sourced and confidence-rated.

[hc-claude-osint](https://github.com/thehipsterciso/hc-claude-osint)

### Conforme
A C++ library for building, consuming, processing, validating, and managing Open
Vulnerability Assessment Language (OVAL) content at submission grade.

Most OVAL tooling optimizes for consumption. Conforme was built for authorship —
treating OVAL content as an engineered artifact rather than a hand-crafted document.
Deterministic. Strict. Unforgiving in the same ways the specification is.

Developed through hands-on OVAL authoring work, including submissions presented
to MITRE and the Center for Internet Security.

[conforme](https://github.com/thehipsterciso/conforme)
